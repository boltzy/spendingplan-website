Keep future money out of reach of everyday spending.

In Household: transfer only what is available to spend now. Leave Future Weeks and Buffer in Household, earmarked separately in Spending Plan. Keep enough there for these reserves and your bills.

In pots on Spending: transfer the cycle funding to Spending, then set Future Weeks and Buffer aside in pots or spaces outside your card-spendable balance. Move the weekly amount into Spending when needed. Your two accounts can be at the same bank or different banks.

What is Buffer for?

Buffer is money set aside for occasional or irregular costs during the cycle, such as a household purchase that doesn’t fit comfortably into a normal week. It is separate from Future Weeks, which holds the money planned for later weekly transfers. You decide when to use Buffer and move the money yourself in your banking app.

A MONTH AT A TIME

Built around
your cycle.

Spending Plan is geared around a monthly allowance, from one cycle start to the next. Your plan divides the spending pool into releases across the days it covers; the first is available at the start.

Choose your usual funding day and the app suggests cycle dates. If that date falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it suggests the preceding Friday. If the day doesn’t exist in a month, it uses the last day of that month first.

Need a different plan for one month? If you’re paid early in December, for example, you can adjust that cycle’s dates and amounts and review the revised weekly releases before starting. Your saved defaults don’t have to change.

Bank holidays and individual payroll rules aren’t automatically accounted for. Check the suggested dates against when your money actually arrives and adjust them if needed.

Getting started

How do I set up my first plan?

On the welcome screen, choose Set up a new plan. Choose where to keep your reserves, then check your funding dates and monthly allowance, including Buffer. On Review your plan, choose whether to Use these as my defaults, then tap Save plan. This creates a Planning draft. When you’re ready to fund it, tap Review bank setup, follow the steps in your banking app and tap Start this cycle. You can run setup again from Settings.

What bank accounts do I need?

Use two separate accounts: one for household income and bills, and one for everyday spending. Two ordinary accounts work. Choose to leave Future Weeks and Buffer earmarked in Household, or keep them in pots or spaces on Spending outside your card-spendable balance. You make all money movements yourself.

Can I change where I keep my reserves?

Choose your setup in the first-plan wizard or Settings → Account setup. Saving a change applies from your next cycle and updates saved future plans. Your active cycle keeps its original setup. Help shows whether its guidance is for the active cycle, a planned cycle or new cycles. When switching setups, check where retained money is held, move it to the right place and count each amount once.

What does “Available at start” mean?

It’s the first allocation, ready to spend when the cycle starts. With Household reserves, transfer this amount to Spending and leave the reserves earmarked in Household. With pots, fund Spending, set aside Future Weeks and Buffer, and leave this first allocation available.

What is Buffer for?

Buffer is a separate reserve for occasional costs. Future Weeks holds money planned for later weekly transfers. Tap Buffer on Active to see what remains and record what you’ve used after moving it in your banking app.

Using your plan

How do I record a weekly transfer?

On Active, tap Review transfer to check the amount and source. Move it from Household or your Future Weeks pot to Spending as shown in the app. Back in Spending Plan, tap Review transfer to record and confirm what you moved. You can record a £0 release without moving money.

Can I change the plan during a cycle?

On Active, tap Top up to bring money forward from one or more future transfers. To change the amount set aside for all remaining transfers, use Adjust active cycle and choose Add to Future Weeks or Take out of Future Weeks. Review the revised schedule before recording the change and make any needed transfer in your bank.

What if I already moved some money before funding day?

Go to Edit this month → Already spent or taken. Use Taken Early for money you’ve already transferred from Household to Spending. Select the week it came from. The amount reduces that week’s allocation and the remaining cycle funding. Bank setup shows the transfer for your chosen account setup. Enter each amount once.

What happens when I complete a cycle?

Completing a cycle opens the next one in Planning. Before funding it, check what remains in Spending, Future Weeks and Buffer, allowing for pending payments. If you keep any of that money for the next cycle, count it once and transfer only what’s still needed.

Dates and transfers

Can I change the dates for just one month?

Open Edit this month → Dates and transfers. Adjust Cycle starts and Cycle ends, then check the dated allocations before saving. For example, if you’re paid early in December, you can use the actual dates without changing your saved defaults. During first setup, the equivalent boundary is Next funding date: the plan ends the day before it.

How are funding dates suggested?

Your Usual funding day suggests monthly dates. For shorter months, the app uses the last day of the month. Weekend dates move to the preceding Friday. Check the suggestions against your actual funding dates, especially around bank holidays.

Can I choose which day my weekly spending money becomes available?

Open Edit this month → Dates and transfers → More timing options. Choose Funding day for an amount at the start and later releases every seven days. Choose Fixed weekday if you want later releases on a weekday you pick. For example, if you’re paid on Friday, the first amount is available then and later releases can fall on Sundays. Short periods are usually allocated less because they cover fewer days.

What do “Release count” and “Plan default” mean?

The Release count includes the first amount available at the start. Plan default uses the preference saved with the plan. In Funding day timing, Automatic works out the count from the dates, and you can choose four or five if the dates allow. With Fixed weekday timing, the count is always automatic.

How are weekly amounts worked out?

The weekly spending pool is divided according to the days covered by each release. Releases are whole pounds: proportional amounts are rounded down and the final release balances the pool. Any sub-pound planning remainder stays in Buffer. The first allocation is available at the start.

Will changing these settings change how much money I have?

Your allowance stays the same when you change timing. The dates, number of allocations and amounts can change. Buffer can also change if you’ve set different targets for four and five allocations. Check the revised schedule before saving.

Offline, iCloud and availability

Does Spending Plan connect to my bank?

No. It does not connect to your bank, track transactions or see live balances. You make transfers in your banking app and record them in Spending Plan.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your plan is stored locally and the app is fully useful offline. You do not need an app account.

What about iCloud?

Private iCloud plan sync is optional and off until you choose to enable it. When enabled, it uses your private iCloud account to sync plans. If versions conflict, they are retained for review rather than silently replacing one another.

Is Spending Plan available on the App Store?

Spending Plan is being prepared for release. Availability and download details will be shared when confirmed.

YOUR PLAN, YOUR BUSINESS

Private by design.

Your plan stays on your device unless you choose to enable private iCloud sync or export a backup. There are no app accounts, analytics, advertising or bank connections.

Exported backups contain financial plan data and are not encrypted by the app. Store them somewhere private. Please don’t include account numbers, passwords or sensitive financial details in support emails.

Optional iCloud sync uses Apple’s private iCloud service. Backups and device reminders are separate from sync.
