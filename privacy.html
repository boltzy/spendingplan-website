SPENDING PLAN

Privacy policy

Spending Plan: Weekly Money is provided by Mark Bolton. Contact spendingplan@boltz.uk.

Your plan

Spending Plan stores the financial information you enter on your device. The app works offline and does not require an app account. It does not connect to your bank, use advertising or include analytics.

Optional iCloud sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, the app stores plan revisions in your private iCloud database so you can access them on your devices using the same Apple Account. Sync is off by default. Conflicting versions are retained for you to review. Apple’s services are subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

You can turn sync off in the app’s settings. This stops further syncing but does not delete existing iCloud copies.

Backups and sharing

You can choose to export a backup or share or print information. Backups contain financial plan information and are not encrypted by Spending Plan. The destination you choose may store or transmit that information under its own terms. Keep exported files somewhere private and delete copies you no longer need.

Reminders

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, Spending Plan schedules notifications on your device. You can change reminder settings in the app and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Support

If you email us for support, we use your message and any screenshot you choose to send to respond to your request. Please avoid sending account numbers, passwords or other sensitive financial details. We normally delete support messages and screenshots once the issue is resolved, unless we need to keep them longer for a specific reason. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you have sent.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

You can turn off iCloud sync in Spending Plan’s settings. This stops further syncing but does not delete existing iCloud copies. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; it does not automatically delete iCloud data, device backups or files you exported elsewhere. Manage those copies separately through Apple’s settings or the service where you saved them. Contact us if you need help understanding these options or wish to request deletion of support information you sent us.
